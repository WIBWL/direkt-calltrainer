# Calltrainer

An AI-powered trainer that helps users improve telephone communication skills through simulated conversations, speech analysis, and qualitative feedback on speaking behavior over time.

## Language

**Session**:
One full pass a user makes through the product: a simulated phone conversation followed by feedback on their communication behavior.
_Avoid_: Call, conversation, training session

**User**:
The person who performs Sessions, practicing their telephone communication skills against an AI-simulated conversation partner. There is no separate human trainer/coach role — feedback is generated by the AI.
_Avoid_: Trainee, learner, customer

**Scenario**:
The situational context configured for a Session — e.g. the caller's reason for calling or goal. Independent of the simulated counterpart's character traits (see Persona).
_Avoid_: Persona, situation

**Persona**:
The character traits of the AI-simulated conversation partner within a Session — e.g. impatient, calm, confrontational. Combined independently with a Scenario.
_Avoid_: Scenario, character, counterpart

**Language**:
The language a Session's simulated conversation is conducted in. Not a Session parameter of its own: it follows from the selected Persona, which carries exactly one language and voice (docs/adr/0043, superseding the earlier per-Session model). Only German is supported so far.
_Avoid_: Locale

**Feedback**:
Qualitative, behavior-focused insight generated by the AI — either scoped to a single Session or aggregated across a User's Sessions over time to surface recurring communication patterns. One term covers both scopes; the scope is a property of a given piece of Feedback, not a separate concept.
_Avoid_: Score, evaluation, result

**Turn**:
One exchange within a Session's simulated conversation — the user's spoken utterance and the Persona's spoken reply to it, treated as a single unit rather than as one Turn per speaker. A Session consists of a sequence of Turns, ordered by a sequence index starting at 1. A Turn may lack one half: the opening Turn has no user utterance, because the Persona speaks first.
_Avoid_: Exchange, round, message

**Transcript**:
The plain, unannotated log of what was said in each Turn of a Session (user text and Persona text), shown to the user after the Session ends. Not AI-analyzed and not to be confused with Feedback.
_Avoid_: Protocol, log, feedback

**Measurement**:
One speech metric measured on a Turn — e.g. speaking rate or intonation. Always describes the user's half of the Turn, never the Persona's: the Persona's speech is synthesized, so measuring it says nothing about how the user spoke. This is what lets a Turn hold both speakers without the attribution becoming ambiguous.
_Avoid_: Metric, score, KPI

**Finding**:
A noteworthy observation at one specific point inside the user's utterance in a Turn — the qualitative counterpart to a Measurement, and like it always about the user's half. Its offset is counted from the start of that utterance, not from the start of the Session.
_Avoid_: Issue, error, annotation
