# ADR 0003: No Human Trainer — Feedback Is Fully AI-Generated

## Context

Classic communication training pairs a trainee with a human coach. Here the User trains alone against an AI counterpart.

## Decision

All Feedback is generated by the system. No human coach is part of the loop.

## Consequences

The product is self-service and scales. Feedback quality depends entirely on the model, and there is no human to correct a misjudgment.
