# ADR 0004: Feedback Is Qualitative, Not Score-Based

## Context

The pilot stakeholder rejected reducing performance to a single number in favour of differentiated, traceable feedback.

## Decision

Feedback is qualitative and behaviour-focused, with concrete improvement suggestions. No overall score or overall colour is shown for a call, a Session or a User. A traffic light on one named classification is allowed only under ADR 0078's conditions.

## Consequences

Feedback is actionable rather than opaque. It is harder to compare at a glance and needs careful wording to stay consistent between runs.
